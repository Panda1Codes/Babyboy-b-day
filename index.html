Happy Birthday, mere Pulkit… mere pattu, mere baccha. 🥹💗 Aaj bas itna chahti hoon ki tum dil se khush raho. Tumhari life mein itni saari achhi cheezein aayein ki tumhe har din muskurane ki koi na koi wajah mil jaaye. Kaash tum ek baar khud ko meri nazron se dekh paate, shayad tab samajh paate ki tum mere liye kitne special ho. Tumhare liye mere paas kitne saare naam hain, Babyboy, baccha, babe, babieee, pattu, chellem. Har naam ke saath ek alag si feeling judi hai: kabhi masti, kabhi pyaar, kabhi tumhe tang karne ka mann, aur kabhi bas tumhari fikr. 🧸💕 Zindagi hamesha humare plan ke hisaab se nahi chalti, aur har kahaani perfect nahi hoti. Lekin aaj kisi sawaal ya pressure ka din nahi hai. Aaj tumhara din hai. Main chahti hoon ki tum hanso, apna favourite khaana khao, cake ka bada sa piece lo, aur thoda sa khud ko bhi celebrate karo. Dua hai ki iss naye saal mein tumhe sukoon mile, tumhare sapne poore hon, tumhari mehnat rang laaye, aur mushkil din bhi tumhe todne ke bajaye aur strong banaayein. Jab tum thak jao, tumhe aaraam mile; jab confused ho, raasta mile; aur jab khush ho, toh woh khushi bahut door tak tumhare saath rahe. Aur ek dua jo mere dil ke bahut kareeb hai, main chahti hoon Bhagwan tumhe duniya ki har achhi cheez de, tumhare saare sapne poore kare, tumhe woh saari khushiyan de jinke tum haqdar ho, aur mujhe tumhara saath de. Main chahti hoon ki humare beech chahe kitne bhi mushkil raaste aayein, hum unse bhaagne ke bajaye saath milkar guzrein. Raasta lamba ho, waqt zyada lage, ya kabhi kadam ladkhada bhi jaayein, bas hum ek dusre ka haath na chhodein. Main nahi chahti ki humari kahaani hamesha aasaan ho, bas itna chahti hoon ki jab bhi zindagi mushkil ho, hum ek dusre ke liye safe place ban sakein. Khushi mein saath hansna, mushkil waqt mein ek dusre ko sambhalna, galti ho toh samajhne ki koshish karna, aur har naye din ek dusre ko phir se choose karna. Mere liye pyaar sirf achhe dinon mein saath rehna nahi, balki mushkil dinon mein bhi patience, respect aur pyaar ke saath ek dusre ka haath thaame rehna hai. Main chahti hoon ki hum jaldi mein kahin pahunchne ki fikr na karein. Bas apni pace par, ek ek kadam karke, aakhri tak saath chal sakein. Chahe kitna bhi waqt lage, chahe kitni baar ruk kar saans leni pade, meri dua hai ki hum dono ek dusre ka haath pakad kar aage badhein. Aur jab kabhi duniya bahut heavy lage, toh hum ek dusre ko yaad dilayein ki hum sab kuch ek hi din mein solve nahi kar sakte, lekin pyaar aur himmat ke saath ek ek din zaroor nikaal sakte hain. 🫶💗 Humari jo bhi achhi yaadein hain, jo hansi, jo comfort aur jo moments mere liye special rahe, main un sabko pyaar se yaad rakhungi. Tum meri kahaani ka ek meaningful hissa rahe ho, aur main aaj tumhare liye sirf achhi cheezein wish karna chahti hoon. Happy Birthday, mere chellem. Aise hi apne silly self rehna, apna khayaal rakhna, aur haan, meri taraf se bhi extra cake kha lena. Kaash main abhi tumhe ek tight sa hug de paati. 🥹🎂💗 Bas meri chhoti si dua hai: tum hamesha khush raho, healthy raho, aur tumhare dil ki achhi wishes poori hon. Janamdin mubarak, pattu. ♡\n\nAur haan, ek baat yaad rakhna, LOVE U LOADSSSSS, mere Babyboy. Itna saara pyaar ki words bhi kam pad jaayein. 💗🫶
